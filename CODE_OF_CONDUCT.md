# Code of Conduct

Everyone is welcome here, no matter their background, identity or experience.

## Please do

- Be friendly and patient
- Assume good intentions
- Give constructive, specific feedback
- Respect that maintainers decide what fits the project

## Please don't

- Insult, harass or discriminate against anyone
- Post spam, advertising or off-topic content
- Share other people's private information

## Enforcement

Comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules can be edited, closed or removed, and repeated violations can lead to a ban from the repository. Report problems to [support@cloaki.de](mailto:support@cloaki.de).
